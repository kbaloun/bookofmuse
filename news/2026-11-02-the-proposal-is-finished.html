The proposal is finished, and the search for an agent is on

November 2, 2026

On November 2 I finished the book proposal — the full document, built for a 230-page, roughly 50,000-word book — and sent it out. Two queries went the same day: one through a web form that unexpectedly demanded the whole proposal before it would let me submit, and one the old-fashioned way, by email.

That makes seven live queries in total. The way this business works, agents reply only if they're interested, sometime in the next six to eight weeks. So the author's oldest job begins: waiting, and writing the next thing while I wait.

No names here — they stay private while the answers are pending.
