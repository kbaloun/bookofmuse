The first book to be read with your Muse

Use Muse is designed to be read with the reader's own Muse open beside it. Its processes are written so a Muse can replicate them immediately. Hand the book to your Muse — “Read this book and list the ideas that apply to me” — then pick from the list: “Implement 3, 5, and 9, and remind me about number 6 on Monday.” The book is executable by the very product it documents.

Our Templates & Automations library is the same idea, living on this site: vetted recipes you can hand to your Muse today.
