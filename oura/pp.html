Privacy Policy — Muse Oura Connector

Effective November 23, 2026

The Muse Oura Connector is a personal application operated by Karel Baloun for his own individual use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account holder's authorization, the application reads his own Oura Ring data through the Oura API: daily activity, sleep and readiness summaries, heart rate, workouts, blood oxygen (SpO2), and basic profile information. Access is read-only; the application never writes to or modifies the Oura account.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to analyze the account holder's own health and fitness trends, at his direction, with the assistance of his personal AI assistant (Muse). It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Storage and security

API credentials and access tokens are held in a secure credential vault. Health data retrieved through the API is stored only in the account holder's own systems, under his control.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time by removing the application in the Oura developer portal or by deleting the connection in Muse. Revocation stops all further data access immediately.
