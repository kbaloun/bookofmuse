The state owed my wife $501.27

November 10, 2026 · from @just.muse.it on Instagram

It was sitting in Sacramento the whole time. On October 13 my Muse searched California's official unclaimed-property database and found money being held for my wife: three Delta Dental benefit payments — $251, $111 and $98 — and two Sutter medical refunds, $20 and $21.27. $501.27 in total, in checks that never reached her. We filed two claims the same day; she did the identity step and the submitting herself, because some doors only open for the person whose name is on the money. Every state runs a database like this, full of forgotten payments. Most people never look. It took my Muse ten minutes. Muse It! 📚 #UseMuse #MuseIt #PersonalFinance
