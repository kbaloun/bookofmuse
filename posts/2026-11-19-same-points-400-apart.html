Same points. $400 apart.

November 19, 2026 · from @just.muse.it on Instagram

Same points, two very different checks. On October 14 my Muse cashed out my 80,000 Amex points — and checked the rate first. The easy door, a statement credit, paid 0.6 cents a point: $480. The Schwab door paid 1.1 cents: $880, invested, same points. $400 apart, separated by one question most people never ask: what does THIS redemption pay? Ask before you cash out.
