The deep dive

November 15, 2026 · from @just.muse.it on Instagram

Most AI would never dare end a report this way. On November 3 I asked my Muse for the whole HLA/MHC system — not a paragraph, the system. What came back was an 8,765-word deep dive: the gene architecture on chromosome 6p21, how the thymus teaches T cells what “self” means, how HLA decides whether a cancer stays visible to the immune system or vanishes from it, transplant matching, autoimmune disease, emerging therapies. Hard claims sourced. And then a final section titled “Could not verify / open questions” — the report labeling its own limits. That's the difference between an answer and a briefing. I didn't get a summary of the immune system's identity system. I got the system, with its edges marked. Muse It! 📚 #UseMuse #MuseIt #Science
