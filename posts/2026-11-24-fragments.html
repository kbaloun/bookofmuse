Fragments

November 24, 2026 · from @just.muse.it on Instagram

Half this book starts as fragments. I dictate in shorthand — one word here, a correction there — and my Muse has learned to read a one-word message as "replace the unclear part of the last one," not as a new thought. It sounds small until you've drafted a whole book that way. Communication isn't bandwidth. It's shared context. Muse It! 📚 #UseMuse #MuseIt
