Ask what changed, not what happened

November 5, 2026 · from @just.muse.it on Instagram

Ask what changed, not what happened. I learned this one the expensive way: my Muse once quoted me a savings total from its own memory of an earlier message, but the log had been rewritten since — the real total was $1,228 higher. Now the house rule is: re-read the file, then do the arithmetic. A summary is a rumor. The diff is the truth. Muse It! 📚 #UseMuse #MuseIt #AITips
