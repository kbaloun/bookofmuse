An author with a Plan B

November 17, 2026 · from @just.muse.it on Instagram

Deadline day, kept. When I queried agents for this book, I set an end to the waiting: a 48-hour warning on November 11, then on Saturday, November 14 I withdrew every query that hadn't picked the book up. Not sour grapes — a plan. Plan B was ready before I needed it: print it myself through KDP and IngramSpark, priced to break even, and let readers decide. An author with a Plan B never has to beg.
