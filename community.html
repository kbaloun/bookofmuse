The community

The book is one person's documented experiment. The community is where it becomes everyone's. Readers use their own Muses to save money, find money, book the trip, start the project, keep in touch — and they share what worked, with the numbers, so the next reader can do it too.

What people share here

Achievements

Something your Muse actually did for you — a saving, a recovery, a booking, a finished chore that had sat parked for months. Numbers and proof welcome; that's the house standard, set by the book's own ledger.

Ideas

A use you invented, a job nobody thought to hand a Muse, a better way to do one of the book's recipes. Good ideas get built into the library — with credit to you, with your permission.

Templates & automations

A recipe, template, or automation you'd like to share with other readers and their Muses. These get the strictest review of all — see the rule below — before they can appear in the library.

The vetting rule — stated plainly, because we mean it.

Nothing submitted to this site is ever published automatically. Every submission is read by a human (and a Muse) first:

  • Ideas are vetted for quality. Only good, vetted ideas go up here.
  • Achievements are checked for the specifics that make them believable — the same receipts standard the book holds itself to.
  • Scripts, templates, and automations are security-validated before posting, without exception — what the script does, what it accesses, what it sends, and what credentials it touches. For security, we do not accept new scripts onto the site without that validation.
  • Your words are yours. We only quote or feature a submission with the submitter's permission, given on the form.

It's slower than an open feed. It's also why you can trust what you find here.

The showcase opens with the book

The community showcase launches alongside the book. Until then, the standard is set by the founding experiment itself: $6,517.83 saved across 19 logged transactions, September 8 – October 18, 2026 — every dollar in a dated ledger. When the showcase opens, reader achievements that meet the same standard will appear here, credited and with permission.
